Good to know

Questions people ask us

Where do I collect the car?

Message us on WhatsApp with your dates. When we confirm the booking, we'll tell you the pickup spot and time.

Does the rate include a driver?

No. Every car we rent is self drive, so you do the driving. We don't offer driver packages.

What do I need to bring?

Your driving licence and an ID proof. Please have both with you when you come to collect the car.

How much is the deposit?

It's refundable and you pay it when you pick up the car. We'll confirm the exact amount on WhatsApp when you book, so there are no surprises on the day.

How is the price worked out?

The rate on each card is for 24 hours. The booking form multiplies it by the number of days to give you an estimate. We confirm the final rate with you on WhatsApp.

Is my booking confirmed once I send the form?

Not quite. The form opens WhatsApp with your details already filled in. Your booking is confirmed once we reply to you.

Which car is right for a family trip?

For up to five people, the Swift or the Baleno will do the job. If you have seven people, or a lot of luggage, go for the Innova or the Innova Crysta.
